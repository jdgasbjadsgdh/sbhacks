SBPE Connection — Mac v2 — 2026.11.02-connectivity.2

Install the complete ZIP with INSTALL.command into your existing Mac SBPE
folder. Existing settings/keys are preserved and replaced files are backed up.
Restart with your usual SBPE launcher when ready. The installer never starts
or restarts the game. The supported Intel client is required; Apple silicon
uses Rosetta. RUN.command can launch a separate extracted installation.

F8: open/collapse Connection; restores Hide all UI.
F9 / Rush hold: arm next-room loading. In SC, arm before the last generator.
F9 again: disarm an unused arm.
Disconnect + hold: beside Rush; queue one disconnect and hold Rush on recovery.
The existing Emergency hold shortcut remains in StarBreak Controls > SBPE.
Preview room: show the current server-selected map while spawn remains held.
WARNING: connect within 10 seconds or you'll be sent to Eschaton.
Connect / F10: continue early loading once, or reconnect from loaded preview.
F10 with an unused arm cancels it without a disconnect.
Free camera: WASD/arrows, Shift faster, while a map preview is held.
Cancel hold: disarm/finish. X: collapse; Hide all UI: hide panels/tabs.
Closing/hiding the panel leaves the hold active.

Rush is the only hold choice. Boss, Timed, 5s refresh and Back to Rush are
absent. The separate Emergency panel was folded into Connection. The main
panel is 328x284 pixels, compared with 368x416 previously. Native multibox
health, Sync, movement and group Rush arming retain their existing behavior;
Emergency activation remains local in the public build.

If a room skips early asset loading, one deferred reconnect tries that gate;
otherwise the panel reports a short world-ready fallback. No periodic refresh
runs. Server deadlines/mission retention are outside the plugin's control.
Offline checks pass; live SC retention after the game update is unverified.
See RUSH AND SC.md for the workflow, protocol stages and limitations.
